## 5. Build sequence — first 5 pages (fastest compounding)

Order chosen on: (a) BOFU-first revenue proximity, (b) open-field ratings, (c) internal-link scaffolding value, (d) coverage of the largest city modifier first so subsequent city pages inherit a linking hub.

1. **P1 — Answering Service Fayetteville AR** *(week 1–2)*. Largest city, most-winnable contested SERP (all incumbents thin/templated). Winning here builds domain topical authority that every later city page borrows. Also the natural target for GBP category alignment.
2. **P2 — Missed Call Text Back NWA** *(week 2–3)*. Cleanest open field (zero local results observed). Doubles as the internal-link hub for every vertical page. Captures the fastest-growing query class (AI/SMS automation terms) [EST].
3. **P4 — After-Hours Answering for NWA Contractors** *(week 3–4)*. Highest-value buyers (emergency jobs), strongest seasonality hook, feeds P2 and later trade variants. Bridges to fort-smith-pros trades.
4. **P3 — Property Management Answering Service NWA** *(week 4–5)*. Directly serves the "Arkansas properties" mandate; low local competition; sticky, high-LTV clients (per-portfolio contracts).
5. **P5 — AI Receptionist Bentonville AR** *(week 5–6)*. Bentonville SERP is unbuilt; vendor-economy density makes it the highest-value-per-capita city; completes the Fayetteville→Bentonville axis and unlocks Rogers/Springdale templated rollouts.

Rationale for compounding: P1+P2 establish the two hub topics (city answering + automation) that all vertical pages (P3/P4/P5) link up to; publishing P1 first means P2–P5 launch with internal links from day one rather than retrofitting. [Strategic estimate.]
